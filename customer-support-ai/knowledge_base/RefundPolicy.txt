TechMart Electronics — Refund & Return Policy
=============================================
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

RETURN WINDOW
-------------
- Hardware/Electronics: 30 days from delivery date.
- Software (boxed): 30 days if unopened.
- Digital downloads/activations: Non-refundable once activated.
- Defective products: 90-day replacement/refund guarantee.

ELIGIBILITY
-----------
Items must be:
1. In original packaging with all accessories.
2. Not physically damaged by the customer.
3. Accompanied by proof of purchase (order number or receipt).
4. Returned within the applicable window.

HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN
------------------------
1. Log in to your TechMart account.
2. Go to Orders → Select the order → Click "Return Item."
3. Choose return reason and preferred resolution (refund or exchange).
4. Print the prepaid return shipping label.
5. Drop off at any UPS or FedEx location.

REFUND TIMELINE
---------------
- Credit/debit card: 5–10 business days after we receive the item.
- PayPal: 3–5 business days.
- Store credit: Instant upon return approval.
- Gift cards: Refunded as store credit only.

EXCEPTIONS
----------
- Special order items: Non-returnable.
- Items marked "Final Sale": Non-returnable.
- Consumables (batteries, ink): Non-returnable if opened.

DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS
------------------
If your product is defective within 90 days:
- Contact us at warranty@techmart.com with photos/video of the defect.
- We will ship a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund.
- Do not ship the item back until we provide a return label.
