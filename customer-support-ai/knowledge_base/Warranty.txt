TechMart Electronics — Warranty Policy
=======================================

STANDARD LIMITED WARRANTY
--------------------------
Duration: 1 year from original purchase date.
Coverage: Defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.

Covered:
  - Manufacturing defects
  - Hardware failures (screen, battery degradation >20%, ports)
  - Pre-installed software issues

NOT Covered:
  - Physical damage (drops, spills, cracks)
  - Unauthorized modifications
  - Normal wear and tear
  - Damage from power surges (use a surge protector)
  - Third-party software issues

EXTENDED WARRANTY OPTIONS
--------------------------
TechMart Care+    — 2 years total — $79 (add within 30 days of purchase)
TechMart Care Pro — 3 years total — $129 (includes 1 accidental damage claim)
TechMart Business Care — 3 years + on-site support — $249

HOW TO CLAIM WARRANTY
----------------------
1. Locate your order number (email or account portal).
2. Email warranty@techmart.com with:
   - Order number
   - Description of defect
   - Photos or video evidence
3. Our team responds within 24 business hours.
4. Approved claims are resolved within 10 business days via:
   - Free repair at authorized service center
   - Replacement unit (refurbished or new)
   - Full refund if repair/replacement is not possible

BATTERY WARRANTY
-----------------
Battery capacity warranted to retain >80% at 500 charge cycles or 1 year.
If battery degrades below 80% within warranty: free battery replacement.

INTERNATIONAL WARRANTY
-----------------------
Warranty is valid in the US, Canada, UK, and EU.
For other regions, contact international@techmart.com.
